IMPORTANT PRIVACY NOTE
“Anonymous to Tomoko” is safer wording than promising absolute anonymity. Avoid asking for names, emails, phone numbers, school information, or other identifying details if you want the form to stay anonymous to you. Jotform may still have its own technical logs/settings.
